Title: Framework 13 Pro - what is missed almost by everybody? (2026)
Author:marcin
When:29 Jul 2026
Taxonomy:English,English blog,x86,modern_laptop

<p>We have seen a lot of hype about new Framework 13 Pro. Various sources
say a lot of good about it and this is true - Framework made huge progress
and new device is really pearl.

<p>But what is missed almost by everybody?
<!--break-->
<p>Laptop has got weight > 1,4kg and cooling, which seems to be not enough
(device is throttling and it doesn't of course mean, that CPU will fail immediately, from the
other hand this problem can make faster replacing motherboard or repair need).
<a href=https://www.notebookcheck.net/Framework-Laptop-13-Pro-review-The-king-of-repairability-is-back.1351246.0.html>The last
problem was notified by Notebookcheck (only?)</a> and it seems to be similar
to the <a href=https://www.notebookcheck.net/Radeon-RX-7700S-performance-debut-Framework-Laptop-16-review.790807.0.html>problem in the Framework 16</a>
(looks like general design flaw from this company).

<p>Next problem is connected with the screen - it seems to be designed specially
for Framework (this probably required a lot of money). And now the rethorical
question - company is advertising producing less e-waste, but it's locking users
to some specified screens. Isn't it opposite to the mission, they try to sell to world?
Wouldn't be better for uses to be able to put into 13 Pro (or 14 Pro) any
13/14" screen? (it could open it for OLED, Low Power screens and many other options)

<p>Please note, that money used for designing new screen could be used for covering
RAM prices increase - company promised it, but was not able to deliver (and after situation
happen Framework didn't change preorder process to make sure, that prices in the preorder will be really
not changed because order will be always done from parts available physically on the stock)

<p>So, currently we have pearl, but also quite heavy and throttling laptop with something looking like
non-standard screen (+big price and quite big standby/idle power usage). I like Framework mission, but...
this American device in various points is worse than devices from Asia and you won't see it
in American reviews.
