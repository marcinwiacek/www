Title: Big Apple laptop chance (2026)
Author:marcin
When:4 Jan 2026
Taxonomy:English,English blog,Apple,x86

In last days I was deeply checking Macbook Air M4 Sky Blue version. You can find many reviews in the Internet, unfortunately they don’t say everything.

<p>First some facts: Apple created very nice looking device with weight 1,24kg. It works without any sound (passive cooling), has got very nice keyboard and perfect touch-pad and offers quite stable 15h / 18h on the 53,8Wh battery (web browsing / video watching).
<!--break-->

<p>Let’s look on the notebookcheck.net review: 

<ol>
<li>0,15W (off) is better than practically all Windows laptops (typically 0,3W or more)
<li>0,27W (standby) is much better too (Windows machines without external graphic need typically 0,6-0,8W)
<li>1,9 – 8,8W (idle) gives mixed feeling (Windows machines can achieve even 1,3 – 4W here) 
</ol>

<p>It looks, that Air has got much better efficiency or optimization, but… screen is power hungry and is eating “amazing” effect. Not only this can be problem:

<ol>
<li>screen doesn’t have PWM (good), but seems to use various levels of dithering (it’s simulating more colors by fast disabling and enabling subpixels)
<li>you cannot buy matt version from Apple (they have nanostructural glass in Macbook Pro, but MBP is using PWM)
<li>MacOS has quite strange scaling and normally in the end you have much lower resolution than physically possible
</ol>

<p>Many people don’t see any problem, but some have to use 3rd party apps like StillColor or matt foil. It doesn’t always work (like for me).

<p>This leads to some questions: what battery times could we see with Low Power low resolution screen? Why can’t we have laptop, which is *really* using all available today technologies for working long without charging?

<p>I’m giving many times my Clevo example, which gave me few years ago 36h: 1kg, 73Wh battery, matt black case, Linux, Low Power Full HD screen and Intel 11gen.

<p>I would like to see at least the same level with Apple Silicon or Lunar Lake.

<p>Current laptops can be very uncomplicated:

<ol>
<li>screen (low power or with all bells and whistles)
<li>keyboard
<li>touch-pad
<li>System on Chip with CPU, RAM, sound card, eventually TPM and controllers
<li>disk (can be in Apple form without controller like in Mac Mini)
<li>battery (makes sense to split it and sell configurations with one or many cells)
<li>Wi-fi
<li>(eventually) camera, card and fingerprint reader, microphone, light sensor, etc.
<li>speakers
</ol>

<p>It must be functional:

<ol>
<li>black or silver case without funny LEDs (I don’t need to see indicator, if laptop is enabled)
<li>no holes on the bottom
<li>minimum one USB-C on the left and right (can be used for charging) added on the small extra board connected with main board with elastic connector
<li>lid with 180 degrees angle
<li>no fan using power from battery (probably we need vapor chamber like in phones)
<li>no RTC battery
<li>(optional) support for Framework modules
<li>must give info about battery wearing or cycles (like in Apple)
<li>disk can be taken out without anything (data security, which could help for example in having one disk for private things and one for corporate work)
<li>if possible, all main settings (power limits, etc.) can be done from BIOS and saved for example on SSD – I want to avoid installing extra software coming from strange sources
<li>small protection against dust and water
</ol>

<p>Everything in this device must be done for energy saving:

<ol>
<li>when you close lid, keyboard and touch-pad should be 100% disabled and don’t take take even 1mW from battery
<li>when user wants to resume from standby, it can be done just with power button (no battery usage from keyboard and touch-pad)
<li>when sound is off, sound card is 100% disabled
<li>it’s possible to control how fast SSD is suspended
<li>TPM, camera, card reader, wifi can be 100% disabled from BIOS (can’t take even 1mW from battery then)
<li>everything on main board can enter S0iX
<li>when device is OFF, only RTC can take power from battery (no more 0,15W please)
</ol>

<p>We don’t know, what Apple will provide in so called “cheap Macbook” with A chip – will it have smaller battery? Or screen like in Macbook Air M1? With dithering? PWM?

<p>Company has got big chance, but will disappoint us or not? It this moment I don’t have big hope, but… maybe… (please note, that I’m not saying anything about Mac OS, which gives me mixed feelings, but in the end works)

<p>I don’t want in the end Windows and Windows device – they are or were crappy for years (only “good enough”) and included a lot of garbage (for example Intel ME, non-working S0iX and x86 debugging modes)

<p>PS. in my personal rating:

<ol>
<li>(excluding screens/display issues and repairability) Macbooks are on the first place
<li>Dell has got only some good reviews (+ tried to be too much Apple) – see video
<li>HP seems to be still acceptable
<li>Lenovo could be interesting, but it comes from China (this can be the most important sometimes) + lives partially in the past (seems to duplicate input devices, etc.)
<li>Clevo – I could buy their device especially after fixes, but it’s not possible anymore (geopolitical situation?)
<li>others (Asus, Acer, MSI, Medion, TongFang, etc.) – no, thank you
</ol>

<p>When we speak about hardware platforms:

<ol>
<li>Apple Silicon or Snapdragon (ARM)
<li>X86 (in laptops Lunar Lake, in desktops AMD)
</ol>

<p>And my software platform list:

<ol>
<li>Linux (but without Gnome) / MacOS
<li>Android / iOS / iPadOS
<li>Windows
</ol>
